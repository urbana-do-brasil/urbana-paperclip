---
title: Choose your language
description: Use Brazilian Portuguese or English without changing your work.
---

Open the account menu in the sidebar and find **Language**. You can also use
the language selector on your profile settings page or the sign-in page.
Choose **Português (Brasil)** or **English**. Brazilian Portuguese is the
default, even when your browser uses English.

With a signed-in account, the choice follows that account across organizations
and browsers. You can choose a language before creating your first organization.
Without an account, the choice stays in this browser for this site. It is kept
separate from signed-in accounts and is not imported when someone signs in.

The open page changes language after saving succeeds. You do not need to reload
the page, and unsaved form contents remain in place. Language changes how
numbers, dates and money are displayed; it does not convert currencies or
rewrite your tasks, names, conversation history or logs.

If saving fails, your previous language stays active. Select a language again
to retry. If loading fails, the application uses your last confirmed choice for
this account, or Portuguese when none is available. Use **Try loading again**
to fetch the saved choice without overwriting it.

Translation is being delivered in stages. Screens outside the initial profile
and language-selection journey may still contain English. Choosing a language
does not mean every product area has already been translated.
